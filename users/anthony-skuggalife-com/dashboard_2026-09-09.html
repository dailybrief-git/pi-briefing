NSW's new statewide Community Participation Plan takes effect, replacing 100+ council rulebooks

From 1 September, a single statewide framework governs how councils and agencies must consult communities on development, sweeping away more than 100 separate local plans. Continues the government's push to standardise and speed up planning approvals - directly relevant to apartment and residential construction timelines.

Energy & data centresRelevance: HighPrimary - Kreisson Pulse

NSW proposes entry bonds and $200k/MW fees to ration data-centre grid connections

The Department of Climate Change, Energy, Environment and Water has put ten grid-reform proposals to comment, including an entry bond and a default $200,000-per-megawatt upgrade fee for data-centre connections in Sydney, Newcastle and Wollongong - a response to up to 28 gigawatts of connection requests. Sits alongside the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Amendment Bill and matters directly for the Macquarie Technology-style data-centre buildout already on Anthony's radar.

▶ Why it matters / what to watch
What
Proposed entry bond + $200k/MW default upgrade fee for data-centre grid connections in three NSW regions
Why
28GW of connection requests is forcing rationing; raises costs for the AI/data-centre infrastructure wave
